
# Category
What gathers various elements together
- A category is a unit that gathers actions, items, properties, skills, and tasks
- The level rises with the experience gained by executing actions
- Long-press to switch between the progress bar and the equipment bar
- One equipment slot is provided per group
___

## Task List
The list of the tasks set on this category
- A task is an event that is achieved when its requirements are met. You claim the reward yourself
- A task whose reward has not been claimed is marked with a ribbon. Open it and press Claim
- You cannot claim a reward while you are at the limit for the number of types you can hold. Make room, then claim it again
