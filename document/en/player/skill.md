
# Skill
Adjusting the battle values for that turn alone when it activates during battle
- A skill is an element that adjusts the battle values for that turn alone when it activates during battle
- Activation is drawn each turn, with at most one per turn for your side and for the opponent. If another skill is drawn first, it does not occur as often as the displayed chance
- Recovery alone does not take effect on that turn; it is applied together in the recovery after the battle is settled
___

## Skill List
The list of the skills this element has
- Lists the skills held by the items of each category
- The percentage is the chance of activation
- The skills held by the action side can be checked in the action column
