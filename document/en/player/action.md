
# Action
The behaviour that can be executed in this category
- An action is something you execute to gain experience and rewards
- The attribute is the category that receives the experience. It can differ from the category the action belongs to, and the level required to execute is judged there as well
- Long-press the execute row to switch between starting and stopping
- The things listed under "Requirement" are consumed on every execution. A percentage is the chance of being consumed
- An action whose unlock requirements are not met is shown with a strikethrough, and its explanation is hidden
___

## Action List
The list of the actions that belong to this category
- An action is something you execute to gain experience and rewards
- The list of actions you can execute in this category
- Long-press to switch whether an action is executing
- Only one action can be executed within a single category
- Depending on the category (standalone set to passive), actions from several categories can progress at the same time
- An action whose unlock requirements are not met is shown with a strikethrough, and its explanation is hidden
___

## Act
Starting and stopping this action and showing its progress
- The ∞ on the far right keeps executing until it stops at the limit of the resources or of the total number of executions
- In the execution order, 0 means execute now and 1 or later means waiting. An interrupted execution keeps its remaining count, returns to waiting, and resumes later
- A reservation goes through even when the queue is full, and the overflow is discarded from the end of the waiting list. A reservation that cannot be executed when its turn comes is discarded as well
- Pressing the target row or the skill row opens that column
