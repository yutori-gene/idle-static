
# Additional Notes
The columns that do not belong to a particular type
- The columns of the events and the groups open on the right from any level.
- The column of the language opens from the column of the settings and from the column of the welcome event.
___

## [_Event_](en/player/event)

## Group
A classification that bundles categories, actions, and items together
- A group is a classification that bundles categories, actions, and items together
- Only one item can be equipped per group
- The value on the right is the level for a category, the required level for an action, and the quantity held for an item
___

## Language
Changing the language used for the display
- Only the language of the interface changes
- Text without a translation is shown in the default language
