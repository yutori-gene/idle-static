
# Character
Checking and operating the state of the protagonist
- The columns lined up here are for looking at the whole across the categories.
- It opens from the character heading in the top column.
___

## Activity
The list of the actions that can be executed, grouped by category
- An action is something you execute to gain experience and rewards
- Long-press a bar to switch execution
- Lv.n is the level required for that action
___

## Inventory
The list of the items you hold, grouped by category
- An item is an element for the things you hold, such as equipment, consumables, and valuables
- Long-press a bar to switch equipment
- Item slots count the number of item types, not the number of items
___

## Selling
Choosing the items you own from a list and selling them all at once
- Only the items you hold that have a selling price are listed
- Selecting one puts your entire quantity up for sale. The value is the total
- Equipment that drops to zero after being sold is unequipped automatically
___

## Status
Showing the current state, such as the corrections applied to a category
- A property is a numeric adjustment that affects the result of executing an action
- Only the adjustments from items are listed here; depending on the action you execute, further adjustments such as skills also apply
- For an item that does not need to be equipped, the adjustment grows in proportion to the quantity held
___

## Ability
The list of the skills that can activate now, grouped by category
- A skill is an element that adjusts the battle values for that turn alone when it activates during battle
- Listed are the skills held by equipped items and by items you hold that do not need to be equipped
- The percentage is the chance of activation
___

## Missions
The list of the tasks of every category and of how far they are achieved
- A task is an event that is achieved when its requirements are met. You claim the reward yourself
- An achieved task is marked with a check. Once achieved, it stays achieved even if the requirements are no longer met
- A task whose reward has not been claimed is marked with a ribbon. Open it and press Claim
