
# Item
The belongings related to this category
- An item is an element for the things you hold, such as equipment, consumables, and valuables
- Only one item can be equipped per group. Items in different groups can be equipped at the same time even within the same category
- The Equip button appears only for an item you hold, whose requirements you meet, and which the category accepts as equipment
- An item whose unlock requirements are not met is shown with a strikethrough, and its explanation and quantity are hidden
___

## Item List
The list of the items that belong to this category
- An item is an element for the things you hold, such as equipment, consumables, and valuables
- The list of items you can hold in this category
- Long-press to switch whether an item is equipped
- Only one item can be equipped per group
- An item whose unlock requirements are not met is shown with a strikethrough, and its explanation and quantity are hidden
- Related shows the actions that obtain the item and the actions that consume it
___

## Trade
Buying and selling this item
- Without a price, the buy and sell slots do not appear at all. Locked items cannot be traded either
- When the item slots are full, you cannot buy a type you do not hold yet. You can still buy more of what you have
- Item slots and queue slots are bought one at a time, and the price rises with each purchase. Some worlds also cap the number of purchases (rewards are not counted)
- Equipment that drops to zero after being sold is unequipped automatically
