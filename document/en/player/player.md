
# Player
The explanation of each column of the play screen
- The screen takes the form of columns increasing from left to right, and the further right you go, the more detailed the content becomes.
- In the initial settings, the same explanation as here is shown as a hint at the bottom of each column.
- The hints can be hidden in the column of the settings.

|Player|Description|
|-|-|
|[_character_](en/player/character)|Checking and operating the state of the protagonist|
|[_category_](en/player/category)|What gathers various elements together|
|[_action_](en/player/action)|The behaviour that can be executed in this category|
|[_item_](en/player/item)|The belongings related to this category|
|[_skill_](en/player/skill)|Adjusting the battle values for that turn alone when it activates during battle|
|[_property_](en/player/property)|The numbers that affect the result of an action|
|[_world_](en/player/world)|Checking and setting the game as a whole|
|[_miscellaneous_](en/player/miscellaneous)|The columns that do not belong to a particular type|
