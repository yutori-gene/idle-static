
# Development
The management of the memos and the errors while the world is developed
- It is used while the world is developed.
- It concerns the development and the testing of this world and has no effect on what is played.
- The player never sees what relates to the development.
___

## Version
The build version number of the editor
- The version number of the editor at the moment the new world was created is recorded automatically.
- It is not updated even when an existing world is loaded and edited.
- The developer cannot change this content.
___

## Memo
The memo record for the developer
- The developer can write it freely as a memo.
- It has no relation to publishing the game.
___

## Errors
The error record of the validation result
- The result of validating the world is saved here automatically.
- For example, it shows as errors things such as a kind of character that cannot be entered, or a number entered over the upper limit.
- When there was not a single error, a message showing success is saved instead, on just one line.
- The developer cannot change this content.
- While errors remain, the world cannot be published. When you operate publish, the validation runs first and stops there if there are errors.
___

## Timestamp
The record of the creation, the saving and the publishing
- The times of the creation, the saving and the publishing of the world are recorded automatically.
- The developer cannot change this content.
___

### Creation Date
The time the world was first created
- It is the date and time when the world was first created.
___

### Save Date
The time the last save was performed
- It is the date and time when the world was last saved.
___

### Publish Date
The time the last publish was performed
- It is the date and time when the world was last published.
___

## Auto Save
Whether the auto save feature is enabled or disabled
- It shows whether the content of the editing is saved to the browser automatically.
- You cannot rewrite this field directly. You change it with the auto save toggle in the menu of the editor.
