
# Component
Common parts
- The common parts used in the settings of various places are pulled out and handled on their own.
- The components used in any of those places are the same, but depending on the place there are fields of a component that are not used.

|Component|Description|
|-|-|
|[_information_](en/editor/information)|The settings for the basic information of an element (ID, name, explanation, icon, and colour)|
|[_requirement_](en/editor/requirement)|The settings for the conditions needed to use or show an element|
|[_acquisition_](en/editor/acquisition)|The settings for gaining items and category levels|
|[_combat_](en/editor/combat)|The settings for the combat corrections on actions of the stamina type|
|[_property_](en/editor/property)|The settings for the effects an item has|
