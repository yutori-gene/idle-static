
# Basic Settings
The basic setting fields for the world as a whole
- Sets the name, the description, the background, the currency, the capacity, the maximum category level, the experience growth rate, the standard combat parameters, and so on of the world.
- These settings affect the whole world.

|Name|Description|
|-|-|
|[_overview_](en/editor/overview)|The basic information of the world and the information of the author|
|[_general_](en/editor/general)|Basic game settings such as the language and the level settings|
|[_design_](en/editor/design)|The look settings of the world, such as the colours and the font|
|[_development_](en/editor/development)|The management of the memos and the errors while the world is developed|
