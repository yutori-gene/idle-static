
# Event
All the events of the world, gathering the tasks, the changed events, and the fixed events
- The events of a world are made up of three kinds: tasks, changed events (changed), and fixed events (unique).
- A task is a mission that becomes achieved when its requirements are met.
- A changed event is an event for internal processing, not shown on the screen, that activates repeatedly with an increase in a quantity as its trigger.
- A fixed event is a slot prepared one per moment, which cannot be added or deleted.

|Kind|Description|
|-|-|
|[_task_](en/editor/task)|A mission that is achieved when the conditions are met|
|[_changed_](en/editor/changed)|An event for internal processing that fires when the quantity of a category, an action or an item increases at once by the amount in the conditions or more|
|[_unique_](en/editor/unique)|The messages and rewards that activate at set moments|
